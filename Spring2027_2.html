Mathematical Methods in Physics, Spring 2027

Syllabus

The schedule is tentative, and I’ll adjust it as the semester unfolds. If there’s anything you’d like me to cover, feel free to email me.

This course develops mathematical methods through explicit calculations in theoretical physics. We begin with Fourier methods and mode expansions, then use the harmonic oscillator to develop path integrals, perturbation theory, and Feynman diagrams. A quantum particle on a circle illustrates how a ’t Hooft anomaly constrains dynamics. We conclude with generalized symmetries, including higher-form and non-invertible symmetries.

Assessment

Seven short in-class quizzes take the place of homework. They are held in the last 10–15 minutes of class in even-numbered weeks. The open-book final exam counts for 50% of the course grade.

Course materials

The course uses lecture notes prepared by the instructor in place of a textbook.

Weekly schedule

  • Week 3 Mode expansions

  • Week 6 Path integral II (Quiz 3)
  • Week 7 Perturbation theory
  • Week 8 Feynman diagrams I (Quiz 4)
  • Week 9 Feynman diagrams II
  • Week 10 Particle on a circle (Quiz 5)
  • Week 11 ’t Hooft anomaly
  • Week 12 Anomaly matching (Quiz 6)
  • Week 13 Generalized symmetries I
  • Week 14 Generalized symmetries II (Quiz 7)

Introduction to Differential Geometry for Physics, Spring 2027

Fun and Joy when Physics Meets Differential Geometry and Topology

物理中的微分几何 · PHYS70019.01 · 3 credits · 54 contact hours

Satoshi Nawata · Department of Physics, Fudan University · Taught in English

Syllabus

This course develops geometry and topology from first principles through concrete problems in physics. Beginning with the Euler characteristic and Kitaev’s toric code, we use homology to explain topological ground-state degeneracy and quantum error correction. We then work from special relativity and electromagnetism toward differential forms, connections, curvature, and Yang–Mills theory.

Assessment

Seven short in-class quizzes take the place of homework. They are held in the last 10–15 minutes of class in even-numbered weeks. The open-book final exam counts for 50% of the course grade.

Course materials

The course uses lecture notes prepared by the instructor in place of a textbook.

Weekly schedule

Part I · Topology and Quantum Matter

  • Week 1 Euler characteristic and surfaces
    Euler's formula V − E + F, triangulations, orientability, genus, χ = 2 − 2g.
  • Week 2 Toric code I (Quiz 1)
    Qubits, Pauli operators, stabilizers, the toric code Hamiltonian and its ground states.
  • Week 3 Toric code II
    Ground-state degeneracy on a torus and on a genus-g surface, logical operators, anyonic excitations (e, m).
  • Week 4 Homology theory (Quiz 2)
    Chain complexes, cycles and boundaries, ℤ₂ homology, Betti numbers, χ = Σ(−1)ᵏ bₖ.
  • Week 5 Homology meets the toric code
    Ground-state degeneracy = |H₁(Σ; ℤ₂)|, a first look at cohomology, quantum error correction.

Part II · Differential Geometry and Gauge Theory

  • Week 6 Special relativity (Quiz 3)
    Lorentz transformations, Minkowski spacetime, tensors and index notation, the field-strength tensor Fμν.
  • Week 7 Differential forms
    Wedge product, exterior derivative, closed and exact forms, de Rham cohomology.
  • Week 8 Maxwell theory in forms (Quiz 4)
    Stokes' theorem, Hodge star, dF = 0 and d⋆F = J, charge conservation.
  • Week 9 Hyperbolic geometry
    Riemannian metrics, Poincaré disk and upper half-plane, geodesics and isometries, the hyperboloid model inside Minkowski space (rapidity space), Gauss–Bonnet and surfaces of genus g ≥ 2.
  • Week 10 Lie groups and Lie algebras (Quiz 5)
    SO(3), SU(2), the Lorentz group SO(1,3) and SL(2,ℂ), PSL(2,ℝ) as hyperbolic isometries, generators, the exponential map, structure constants, representations.
  • Week 11 Gauge symmetry
    U(1) gauge transformations, potential as a connection, Aharonov–Bohm effect, Dirac monopole and the first Chern number.
  • Week 12 Connections and curvature (Quiz 6)
    Fiber bundles, covariant derivative, curvature as field strength.
  • Week 13 Yang–Mills theory I
    Non-abelian field strength, Bianchi identity, Yang–Mills equations and action.
  • Week 14 Yang–Mills theory II (Quiz 7)
    Yang–Mills in the Standard Model, instantons and topological charge, where geometry and topology meet.
